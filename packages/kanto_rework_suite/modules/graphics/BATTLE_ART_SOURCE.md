# KRS Battle Art source

Imported from the user-supplied `battleanimated.zip` for local KRS integration.

The archive itself states that generated Pokémon artwork is not covered by the source mod license. Redistribution rights for the artwork are therefore **not established by this package** and must be cleared before public redistribution.

KRS does not copy third-party runtime code. Atlas segmentation and runtime selection are implemented independently in KRS.
